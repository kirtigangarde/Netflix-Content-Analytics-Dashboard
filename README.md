## Project Overview
This project is a complete Data Analytics and Business Intelligence dashboard developed using **Python** and **Power BI** on the Netflix Movies and TV Shows dataset.

The project focuses on analyzing Netflix content based on:
- Genres
- Countries
- Content Ratings
- Movies vs TV Shows
- Year-wise Content Growth
- Audience Categories

The dataset was cleaned and preprocessed using Python in Jupyter Notebook, and interactive visualizations were created using Power BI.

---

## Objectives
- Analyze Netflix content distribution
- Identify the most popular genres
- Analyze content growth over years
- Understand country-wise contribution of Netflix content
- Study audience rating distribution
- Build an interactive business intelligence dashboard

---

## Dashboard Insights

### Key Findings
- Movies dominate Netflix content compared to TV Shows.
- Netflix content rapidly increased after 2015.
- United States contributes the highest amount of content.
- Drama and Comedy are among the most popular genres.
- TV-MA and TV-14 are the most common audience ratings.
